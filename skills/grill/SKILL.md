`grill` is now `brainstorm --grill`. This alias works through 0.4.x and is removed in 0.5.0.

Run the `brainstorm` skill with `--grill` (`/goodfellow:brainstorm --grill $ARGUMENTS`) and follow
it exactly, including its tier step. Tell the operator once, in one line, that the new form is
`/goodfellow:brainstorm --grill`.
